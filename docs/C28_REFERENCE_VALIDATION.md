## MATLAB R2024a comparison

Follow [START_C28.md](../START_C28.md). The standalone function discovers exactly
one study index or accepts an explicit relative path. It verifies contract,
completion receipts and the three consumed artifact hashes without loading large
energy-event files or simulation.mat. It checks completed execution, same release,
model/driver, solver, registry, assumptions, pinned packet identities and compatible
reference source hashes. Reference provenance is bound by the package manifest;
CSV and copied source hashes are also checked at runtime.

Candidate temperatures are validated as finite, strictly increasing samples,
covering exactly the evaluation endpoints. Their continuous air temperatures are
linearly interpolated onto native reference timestamps, with **no extrapolation**.
Reference sampling must be complete and uniform; missing/duplicate reference
samples fail. Candidate sampling is adaptive, not a presumed uniform 30 s export;
its count and maximum gap are recorded, and no undocumented gap threshold is
invented. Duplicate/nonfinite candidate samples fail. Both cases use the same
40,321 reference timestamps, avoiding adaptive-output density weighting.

Outputs in a new unique directory under outputs/boptest/c28:

- temperature_comparison.png/.fig: black BOPTEST air, blue no solar, red solar.
- temperature_errors.png/.fig: signed candidate-minus-reference error.
- metrics.csv: MAE, RMSE, maximum absolute error, bias and valid sample count.
- daily_metrics.csv: 14 evaluation-day bins; final endpoint belongs to day 13.
- comparison_provenance.json: input hashes, execution release and sampling counts.
- validation_report.md: measured metric changes, worst sampled-error times,
  interpretation limits and next engineering recommendation.

Negative solar-minus-no-solar MAE/RMSE change means improvement. A lower signed
bias alone does not mean improvement; assess its magnitude and other metrics.
Worst sampled errors are not bounds between samples. No assumed accuracy
threshold, calibration or automatic physical-cause attribution is introduced.

## Evidence, hypotheses and remaining blocker

This compares reduced thermal-zone response under **prescribed BOPTEST radiator
heat**, not independent hydronic-system prediction or real-world validation.
Initial-state reduction, omitted window/airflow physics and solar allocation
remain possible explanations, not measured causes. Review temporal errors,
controlled C2.7 convergence and event energy closure before selecting subsequent
physics work. No C2.9 work has started.
